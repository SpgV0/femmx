FEMM copy-function redraw benchmark
====================================
Base model size:           1601 block labels
Copy actions per run:      30 (separate mi_copytranslate calls)
Trials per side:           3 (best of, interleaved)

A) Baseline (redraw after every copy):      0.453 s  (15.1 ms/copy)
B) mi_setredraw(0)/(1) around the batch:    0.017 s  (0.6 ms/copy)

Speedup: 25.90x

All trials (s):
  baseline:   0.453, 0.462, 0.471   (spread 4%)
  suppressed: 0.023, 0.017, 0.018   (spread 33%)

The timings above are trend data, not a pass/fail SLA -- see the
comment on MAX_ACCEPTABLE_SLOWDOWN. The machine-readable copy is
copy_benchmark_trend.json, uploaded as a CI artifact.
